## Official Provision
*   **Act:** Income-tax Act, 2025
*   **Section:** 20
*   **Chapter:** IV (Computation of total income)
*   **Effective date:** As specified by the Central Government via notification.

Section 20 establishes the chargeability of the annual value of property consisting of buildings or land appurtenant thereto to tax under the head "Income from house property." It further provides an exemption for property used by the owner for their own business or profession.

## In Simple Language
This section states that if you own a building or land attached to a building, the annual value of that property is considered taxable income. However, if you use any part of that property to run your own business or profession, that specific portion is excluded from this tax head.

## Key Points
*   The tax applies to the "annual value" of the property.
*   The property must consist of buildings or lands appurtenant (attached/belonging) thereto.
*   The assessee must be the owner of the property for it to be taxable under this head.

## Who Does This Apply To?
This applies to any assessee who owns property consisting of buildings or lands appurtenant thereto, provided the property is not used for the assessee's own business or profession.

## Conditions
*   The property must be owned by the assessee.
*   The property must consist of buildings or land appurtenant to buildings.

## Exceptions
The provisions of sub-section (1) do not apply to portions of the property occupied by the assessee for their business or profession, provided the profits from that business or profession are chargeable to income-tax.

## Practical Example
*Disclaimer: This example assumes figures for illustrative purposes only and does not represent legal limits.*
If an individual owns a building where the ground floor is used as a shop (business) and the upper floor is rented out, only the annual value of the upper floor is chargeable under "Income from house property." The portion used for the shop is excluded under Section 20(2).

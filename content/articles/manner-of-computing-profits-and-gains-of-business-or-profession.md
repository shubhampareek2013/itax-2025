## Official Provision
- Act: Income-tax Act, 2025
- Section: Section 27
- Chapter: Chapter IV — Computation of total income (Part: Profits and gains of business or profession)
- Effective date: As per the official provisions of the Income-tax Act, 2025.

Section 27 mandates that the income referred to in section 26 must be computed in accordance with the provisions of sections 28 to 60, with the specific exclusion of section 58.

## In Simple Language
When calculating the specific income mentioned under section 26, you must follow the rules set out in sections 28 through 60, but you must leave out section 58 entirely from that computation process.

## Key Points
- Applies to the computation of income referred to in section 26.
- Requires computation to follow sections 28 to 60.
- Explicitly excludes section 58 from this group of sections.

## Who Does This Apply To?
This applies to taxpayers who are required to compute the income referred to in section 26 under Chapter IV of the Act.

## Conditions
- The income being computed must be the income referred to in section 26.
- The computation must strictly adhere to sections 28 to 60 while bypassing section 58.

## Exceptions
- Section 58 is excluded from the computation rules governing the income referred to in section 26.

## Official Provision
* **Act:** Income-tax Act, 2025
* **Section:** 8
* **Chapter:** Chapter II — Basis of charge
* **Effective date:** 1-4-2026 (substituted by the Finance Act, 2026)

Section 8 provides that when a specified person receives a capital asset, stock-in-trade, or both from a specified entity during a tax year upon its dissolution or reconstitution, the entity is deemed to have transferred such items in that year. The resulting profits and gains are deemed to be the income of the specified entity for that tax year and are chargeable to income-tax under "Profits and gains of business or profession" or "Capital gains". The fair market value of the asset or stock-in-trade on the receipt date is deemed to be the full value of the consideration for the deemed transfer.

## In Simple Language
When a partnership firm, association of persons (AOP), or body of individuals (BOI) gives a capital asset or stock-in-trade to a partner or member because the firm or entity is being dissolved or reconstituted, the law treats it as if the firm or entity sold that asset. The firm or entity must pay tax on the profits or gains arising from this deemed sale in the year the asset is received. The full value of consideration for this deemed transfer is considered to be the fair market value of the asset on the date of its receipt.

## Key Points
* Applies when a specified person receives a capital asset or stock-in-trade from a specified entity.
* Triggered in connection with the dissolution or reconstitution of the specified entity.
* The specified entity is deemed to have transferred the capital asset or stock-in-trade in the year of receipt.
* Resulting profits and gains are deemed to be the income of the specified entity of that tax year.
* Taxable under "Profits and gains of business or profession" or "Capital gains".
* Fair market value on the date of receipt is deemed to be the full value of the consideration received or accruing from the deemed transfer.
* The Board, with the previous approval of the Central Government, may issue guidelines to remove difficulties arising in giving effect to this section and section 67(10).
* Guidelines must be laid before each House of Parliament for a total period of thirty days and are subject to modification or annulment by Parliament.

## Who Does This Apply To?
* **Specified Entity:** A firm, or other association of persons, or body of individuals (excluding a company or a co-operative society).
* **Specified Person:** A person who is a partner of a firm or a member of another association of persons or body of individuals (excluding a company or a co-operative society) in any tax year.

## Conditions
* Receipt of a capital asset or stock-in-trade (or both) by a specified person from a specified entity.
* The receipt must be in connection with the dissolution or reconstitution of the specified entity.

## Exceptions
* Companies and co-operative societies are explicitly excluded from the definitions of "specified entity" and "specified person".

## Practical Example
*Note: The following amounts are illustrative assumptions and do not represent statutory limits.*
Suppose a partnership firm (a specified entity) is reconstituted when a new partner is admitted. In connection with this reconstitution, the firm distributes a capital asset to an existing partner (a specified person). Under Section 8, the firm is deemed to have transferred the capital asset in that tax year, and the fair market value of the asset on the date of receipt is deemed to be the full value of the consideration for computing the firm's capital gains or business profits.

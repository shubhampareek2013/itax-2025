## Official Provision
- Act: Income-tax Act, 2025
- Section: 2
- Chapter: Chapter I — Preliminary
- Effective Date: April 1, 2026 (as enacted by the Parliament)

Section 2 outlines the definitions of various terms, expressions, authorities, and legal concepts—such as "assessee", "agricultural income", "capital asset", "company", "dividend", "income", "short-term capital asset", "transfer", and "virtual digital asset"—unless the context otherwise requires.

## In Simple Language
This section acts as a dictionary for the entire Income-tax Act, 2025. When words like "income", "company", "transfer", or "assessee" appear in other sections of the law, their meanings are governed by the specific clauses set out in this section.

## Key Points
- Contains over 110 definitions governing terminology used across the statute.
- Defines core tax concepts including "total income", "tax", "assessment", and "previous/tax year related terms".
- Defines specific corporate actions such as "amalgamation", "demerger", and "slump sale".
- Details what constitutes "agricultural income", "capital asset", "short-term capital asset", and "virtual digital asset".
- Explores inclusive and exhaustive descriptions of "person", "income", and "dividend".

## Who Does This Apply To?
Every taxpayer, assessing authority, appellate body, and professional interpreting or applying the Income-tax Act, 2025.

## Conditions
The definitions apply throughout the Act "unless the context otherwise requires," meaning a specific section may modify or restrict how a term applies within its own domain.

## Exceptions
Terms may carry a different meaning if a specific provision explicitly provides an alternative definition or if the statutory context of another chapter requires a different interpretation.

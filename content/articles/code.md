## Official Provision
- **Act:** Income-tax Act, 2025
- **Section:** 10
- **Chapter:** Chapter II — Basis of charge
- **Effective date:** As per the general commencement date of the Act.

This section provides special rules for assessing the income of husbands and wives governed by the community of property system ("COMMUNIAO DOS BENS" under the Portuguese Civil Code of 1860) in specified regions. It prevents their total income under any head from being assessed together as an association of persons or body of individuals, mandates an equal split for non-salary incomes, and assigns salary income exclusively to the earning spouse.

## In Simple Language
When a husband and wife live in Goa, Dadra and Nagar Haveli, or Daman and Diu and are governed by the specific Portuguese civil code community of property system, the tax department does not tax them together as a single group. Instead, all income other than salaries is divided right down the middle, with half included in the husband's total income and half in the wife's. However, income earned under the head "Salaries" is not split; it is taxed entirely in the hands of the spouse who actually earned it.

## Key Points
- Prevents joint assessment of husband and wife as an association of persons or body of individuals under the community of property system.
- Applies to the State of Goa and the Union territories of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu.
- Applies to the community of property system known as “COMMUNIAO DOS BENS” under the Portuguese Civil Code of 1860.
- Non-salary income under each head of income is divided equally between the husband and the wife.
- Divided income is included separately in each spouse's total income, and the remaining provisions of the Act apply accordingly.
- Income under the head “Salaries” is included solely in the total income of the spouse who actually earned it.

## Who Does This Apply To?
Husbands and wives who are governed by the community of property system (“COMMUNIAO DOS BENS” under the Portuguese Civil Code of 1860) in force in the State of Goa and the Union territories of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu.

## Conditions
- The taxpayers must be a husband and wife governed by the specified community of property system ("COMMUNIAO DOS BENS" under the Portuguese Civil Code of 1860).
- The community of property system must be in force in the State of Goa or the Union territories of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu.

## Exceptions
- Income under the head “Salaries” is excluded from equal division; it must be included in the total income of the spouse who actually earned it.

## Practical Example
*Disclaimer: Practical examples use illustrative assumptions only and do not represent legal limits or fixed thresholds.*
Suppose a husband and wife in Goa operate a business generating ₹5,000,000 in income, and the husband also earns a salary of ₹1,200,000. Under Section 10, the business income is divided equally, meaning ₹2,500,000 is included in the husband's total income and ₹2,500,000 in the wife's total income. The salary of ₹1,200,000 is not split and is fully included in the husband's total income because he actually earned it.

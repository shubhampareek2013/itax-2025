## Official Provision
* Act: Income-tax Act, 2025
* Section: 16
* Chapter: Chapter IV — Computation of total income (Part: Salaries)
* Effective date: As per the official provisions of the Income-tax Act, 2025.

This section defines the scope and components of "salary" for the purposes of Part (Salaries) under Chapter IV, listing out various receipts, payments, and contributions that are to be included as salary income.

## In Simple Language
Section 16 sets out the comprehensive list of payments and benefits that qualify as "salary" for tax purposes. If an employee receives any of the specified items—such as wages, pension, gratuity, perquisites, advance salary, or specified employer contributions—they fall within the definition provided by this section.

## Key Points
* Includes traditional forms of remuneration like wages, annuities, pensions, gratuities, fees, and commissions.
* Covers perquisites and profits in lieu of or in addition to salary or wages.
* Includes advance salary payments and payments for unavailed leave.
* Encompasses taxable annual accretions to recognized provident funds and transferred balances as per Schedule XI.
* Includes employer contributions under specific pension schemes (referred to in section 124) and Central Government contributions to the Agniveer Corpus Fund (referred to in section 125).

## Who Does This Apply To?
This applies to individuals receiving remuneration or payments categorized as salary under this Part, including employees and individuals enrolled in specific schemes like the Agnipath Scheme.

## Conditions
* The payments, perquisites, contributions, or accretions must fall under clauses (a) through (l) of this section.
* Provident fund items must meet the extent of chargeability specified under Schedule XI.
* Contributions to pension schemes or the Agniveer Corpus Fund must correspond to sections 124 and 125 respectively.

## Exceptions
No specific exceptions are provided within the text of this section.

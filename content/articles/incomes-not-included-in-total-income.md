## Official Provision
- Act: Income-tax Act, 2025
- Section: 11
- Chapter: Chapter III — Incomes which do not form part of total income
- Effective date: As per the provisions of the Income-tax Act, 2025.

Section 11 provides that specific incomes listed in Schedules II, III, IV, V, and VI shall not be included when computing a person's total income for a tax year, provided the conditions specified within those Schedules are met. If those conditions are not satisfied in any tax year, the income becomes chargeable to tax under the Act. Similarly, persons enumerated in Schedule VII are not chargeable to tax on their total income for a tax year subject to fulfilling specified conditions, failing which their income is charged to tax under the provisions of the Act. Additionally, the Central Government is empowered to make rules or issue notifications for the purposes of this section as specified in the relevant Schedules.

## In Simple Language
Section 11 acts as a gateway provision linking the exclusion of certain incomes and persons from taxation to specific Schedules (Schedules II through VII). If you meet the conditions laid down in those Schedules, your specified income or person status is protected from being taxed. If you fail to meet those conditions during any tax year, the protection lapses, and the income becomes subject to tax under the Act.

## Key Points
- Incomes enumerated in Schedules II, III, IV, V, and VI are excluded from total income if conditions in the respective Schedules are met.
- If the conditions for these scheduled incomes are breached in any tax year, the income is charged to tax for that tax year.
- Persons enumerated in Schedule VII are not chargeable to tax on their total income for a tax year if they fulfill the specified conditions.
- If the conditions for persons under Schedule VII are not met in any tax year, their income is charged to tax under the Act.
- The Central Government is empowered to make rules or issue notifications for this section as specified in Schedules II, III, IV, V, VI, and VII.

## Who Does This Apply To?
This section applies to any person whose income is enumerated in Schedules II, III, IV, V, and VI, or who is enumerated as a person in Schedule VII under the Income-tax Act, 2025.

## Conditions
- Fulfillment of the specific conditions laid down in Schedules II, III, IV, V, and VI for the exclusion of enumerated incomes.
- Fulfillment of the specific conditions laid down in Schedule VII for persons to be exempt from being chargeable to tax on their total income.

## Exceptions
- If the conditions specified in the respective Schedules (II to VII) are not satisfied in any tax year, the exemption/exclusion does not apply, and the income is charged to tax under the Act.

## Practical Example
*Disclaimer: The following is an illustrative assumption and not a legal limit.*
Suppose an entity earns income enumerated under Schedule II and complies with all conditions prescribed in that Schedule for the relevant tax year. Under Section 11(1), that income is not included in computing its total income. However, if the entity violates a prescribed condition during a subsequent tax year, Section 11(2) dictates that the income will be charged to tax for that tax year.

## Official Provision
- Act: Income-tax Act, 2025
- Section: Section 18
- Chapter: Chapter IV — Computation of total income (Part: Salaries)
- Effective date: As per the official provisions of the Income-tax Act, 2025.

Section 18 defines "profits in lieu of salary" for the purposes of the Salaries part, which includes certain compensation for termination or modification of employment, lump sum amounts received before joining or after cessation of employment, and specific payments from employers, funds, or Keyman insurance policies. Sub-section (2) excludes certain specified payments referenced in Schedule II and Schedule III from the scope of payments referred to in sub-section (1)(c).

## In Simple Language
Section 18 explains what types of extra payments from an employer, former employer, or other persons are treated as "profits in lieu of salary" and therefore taxed under the head of salaries. This includes compensation for losing a job or changing job terms, payments received before starting or after leaving a job, and certain payouts from provident funds (excluding the employee's own contributions and interest thereon) or Keyman insurance policies (including bonuses). However, certain specific payments listed in the schedules are carved out and not treated as profits in lieu of salary under sub-section (1)(c).

## Key Points
- "Profits in lieu of salary" includes compensation due or received from an employer or former employer connected with the termination of employment or modification of employment terms.
- It includes amounts due or received (in lump sum or otherwise) from any person before joining their employment or after ceasing employment with them.
- It includes certain payments from an employer, former employer, or provident/other funds (excluding employee contributions and interest on those contributions).
- It covers sums received under a Keyman insurance policy (including allocated bonuses), as defined in Schedule II.
- Certain payments listed under Schedule II (Table: Sl. No. 3, Sl. No. 4, Sl. No. 8) and Schedule III (Table: Sl. No. 11) are excluded from the operation of sub-section (1)(c).

## Who Does This Apply To?
This section applies to assessees who receive compensation, lump-sum amounts, fund payouts, or Keyman insurance proceeds in connection with employment, former employment, prospective employment, or cessation of employment.

## Conditions
- The amounts must fall within the categories described in sub-section (1)(a), (b), or (c) (such as termination compensation, pre-joining or post-cessation payments, fund payouts excluding employee contributions/interest, or Keyman insurance proceeds).
- The payments must not fall under the specific exclusions listed in sub-section (2).

## Exceptions
- Payments referred to in sub-section (1)(c) do not include payments referred to in:
  - Schedule II (Table: Sl. No. 3)
  - Schedule II (Table: Sl. No. 4)
  - Schedule II (Table: Sl. No. 8)
  - Schedule III (Table: Sl. No. 11)

## Practical Example
*Practical Example (Illustrative)*
Suppose an individual receives a lump-sum compensation payment from their former employer upon the termination of their employment contract. Under Section 18(1)(a), this compensation is classified as "profits in lieu of salary." If another individual receives a payout from a provident fund consisting entirely of their own historical contributions and accumulated interest thereon, that specific portion is evaluated against the statutory definitions and relevant schedule exclusions provided in the Act.

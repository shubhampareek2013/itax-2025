## Official Provision
- Act: Income-tax Act, 2025
- Section: 24
- Chapter: Chapter IV — Computation of total income (Part: Income from house property)
- Effective date: As per the official provisions of the Income-tax Act, 2025.

Section 24 specifies that when a property is co-owned with definite and ascertainable shares, the co-owners will not be assessed as an association of persons. Instead, their income is computed separately under this Part according to their respective shares and included in their individual total incomes, and the relief under section 21(6) applies as if each co-owner is individually entitled to it.

## In Simple Language
If multiple people own a house property together and their individual shares are clearly defined and known, the tax department will not treat them as a group or association of people for tax purposes. Each person's share of the income from the property is calculated separately and added to their own total income. Furthermore, any relief under section 21(6) is given to each co-owner individually as if they each qualify for it on their own.

## Key Points
- Applies to property that is co-owned.
- Co-owners must have definite and ascertainable shares.
- Co-owners are not assessed as an association of persons.
- Income is computed separately according to each co-owner's respective share.
- Each co-owner's computed share is included in their total income.
- Relief under section 21(6) is available as if each co-owner is individually entitled to it.

## Who Does This Apply To?
- Co-owners of a property whose shares are definite and ascertainable.

## Conditions
- The property must be co-owned.
- The shares of the co-owners must be definite and ascertainable.

## Exceptions
- None specified in the text.

## Practical Example
*Practical Example (For illustrative purposes only)*
Suppose a residential property is co-owned by two individuals with exactly equal and ascertainable shares of 50% each. Under Section 24, they are not treated as an association of persons. The income from the property is computed separately for each individual based on their 50% share and included in their respective total incomes. Additionally, relief under section 21(6) applies to each co-owner individually.

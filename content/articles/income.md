## Official Provision
- **Act:** Income-tax Act, 2025
- **Section:** Section 14
- **Chapter:** Chapter IV — Computation of total income
- **Effective date:** As per the official provisions of the Income-tax Act, 2025.

Section 14 provides that, irrespective of anything to the contrary in the Act, no deduction is allowed for expenditure incurred in relation to income that does not form part of total income. If the Assessing Officer is not satisfied with the assessee's claim regarding such expenditure or the claim that no expenditure was incurred, the officer shall determine the expenditure amount in accordance with a prescribed method. Furthermore, these provisions apply even if the exempt income has not accrued, arisen, or been received during that tax year.

## In Simple Language
If you earn income that is exempt from tax (i.e., it does not form part of your total income), you cannot claim deductions for any expenses you spent to earn that income. If the Assessing Officer doubts your calculations or your claim that you spent nothing to earn that tax-free income, the officer has the authority to calculate the disallowable expenditure using a prescribed method. This rule applies regardless of whether the exempt income was actually received or accrued during the same tax year.

## Key Points
- No deduction is permitted for expenditure incurred in relation to income that does not form part of total income.
- The Assessing Officer may intervene if unsatisfied with the correctness of the assessee's claimed expenditure or the claim that no expenditure was incurred.
- The Assessing Officer determines such expenditure according to a prescribed method.
- The provision applies even when the relevant income has not accrued, arisen, or been received in that tax year.
- Operates notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in the Act.

## Who Does This Apply To?
This applies to any assessee who incurs expenditure in relation to income that does not form part of total income under the Act.

## Conditions
- The expenditure must be incurred in relation to income which does not form part of total income.
- The Assessing Officer must not be satisfied with the correctness of the assessee's claimed expenditure or the claim that no expenditure was incurred in relation to such income.

## Exceptions
The official text does not specify any exceptions to this section.

## Practical Example

Under Section 14, those administrative expenses cannot be deducted when computing the assessee's total income. If the Assessing Officer is unsatisfied with how the assessee accounts for these expenses, the officer determines the disallowable amount using the prescribed method.

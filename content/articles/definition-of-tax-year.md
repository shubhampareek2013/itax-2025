## Official Provision
- **Act:** Income-tax Act, 2025
- **Section:** Section 3
- **Chapter:** Chapter I — Preliminary
- **Effective Date:** As per the Act

Section 3 defines the term "tax year" for the purposes of the Act as the twelve-month period of the financial year commencing on the 1st of April. It further provides that for a newly set up business or profession, or a source of income newly coming into existence during a financial year, the tax year begins on the date of setup or the date the source comes into existence and ends with that financial year.

## In Simple Language
The "tax year" is normally a twelve-month period starting on April 1st. However, if a business, profession, or a new source of income is started partway through a financial year, the tax year for that specific case does not start on April 1st. Instead, it starts on the exact date the business was set up or the income source came into existence, and it ends on the last day of that financial year (March 31st).

## Key Points
- "Tax year" means the twelve-month period of the financial year commencing on the 1st of April.
- For newly set up businesses or professions, the tax year begins on the date of setting up.
- For a source of income newly coming into existence in any financial year, the tax year begins on the date on which such source comes into existence.
- In both exception cases, the tax year ends with the said financial year.

## Who Does This Apply To?
This applies to all taxpayers under the Income-tax Act, 2025 who are required to determine their tax year, including persons running businesses, practicing professions, or deriving income from newly created sources during a financial year.

## Conditions
- For general taxpayers, the tax year must be the twelve-month period of the financial year commencing on the 1st of April.
- For newly set up businesses, professions, or newly existing income sources, the period must begin on the specific date of setup or emergence and end with the financial year.

## Exceptions
The standard twelve-month financial year starting on April 1st is modified in the case of a business or profession newly set up, or a source of income newly coming into existence in any financial year, where the tax year instead begins on the specific date of setup or emergence.

If an individual sets up a new consulting business on October 15th of a financial year, their tax year for that business does not begin on April 1st of that year. Instead, under Section 3(2), their tax year begins on October 15th and ends on March 31st of that financial year.

## Official Provision
- Act: Income-tax Act, 2025
- Section: Section 17
- Chapter: Chapter IV — Computation of total income (Part: Salaries)
- Effective date: As per the official text implementation framework.

Section 17 defines the term "perquisite" for the purposes of the Salaries part, detailing specific items included such as rent-free or concessional accommodation, specified securities, sweat equity shares, employer obligations paid, certain insurance and provident fund contributions, and annual accretions. It also outlines specific exemptions and exclusions relating to medical treatment, health insurance, and transport, along with necessary definitions for terms like fair market value, family, specified security, and sweat equity shares.

## In Simple Language
Section 17 explains what extra perks, benefits, or facilities (known as perquisites) provided by an employer to an employee must be counted as part of their taxable salary. It also lists specific items—such as certain medical treatments and health insurance payments—that are excluded from being treated as taxable perquisites. Finally, it provides clear definitions for specialized terms used throughout the section.

## Key Points
- Includes rent-free or concessional accommodation provided to the assessee.
- Includes benefits or amenities provided to specified directors or employees meeting prescribed monetary thresholds.
- Includes specified securities or sweat equity shares allotted or transferred free of cost or at a concessional rate.
- Covers employer payments for obligations that would otherwise be payable by the employee.
- Includes certain life assurance, annuity, and excess provident fund or superannuation fund contributions (aggregate contributions exceeding ₹7,500,000 in a tax year), along with annual accretions.
- Provides specific exclusions for medical treatment in employer-maintained or approved hospitals, health insurance premiums under approved schemes, and specific travel/medical expenses abroad subject to conditions.

## Who Does This Apply To?
- Employees who receive perquisites, benefits, or amenities from their current or former employers.
- Employers providing accommodation, shares, securities, or meeting employee obligations.
- Specified directors and employees whose monetary salary exceeds prescribed thresholds for specific benefits.

## Conditions
- Valuation of accommodations, specified securities, sweat equity shares, and annual accretions must be computed in the prescribed manner.
- Excess employer contributions to specific funds are limited to the ₹7,500,000 threshold per tax year.
- Exclusions for medical treatment and travel abroad are subject to restrictions such as RBI limits and gross total income limits for travel expenses.

## Exceptions
- Medical treatment provided in hospitals maintained by the employer, Government, local authority, or approved hospitals.
- Insurance premiums paid by the employer or employee for health insurance under approved schemes.
- Expenditure incurred by the employer for transporting the assessee between residence and office or workplace.
- Medical treatment and stay abroad for the employee or family members, and travel for one attendant, to the extent permitted by the Reserve Bank of India and subject to employee gross total income limits for travel.

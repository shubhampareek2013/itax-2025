## Official Provision
* **Act:** Income-tax Act, 2025
* **Section:** 7
* **Chapter:** Chapter II — Basis of charge
* **Effective date:** As applicable under the Income-tax Act, 2025.

Section 7 details specific streams of income that are deemed to be received within a tax year for the purposes of inclusion in total income. These include annual accretions and transferred balances relating to recognized provident funds under specified provisions, employer contributions under a pension scheme mentioned in section 124, and dividends (including interim dividends) declared, distributed, paid, or unconditionally made available by a company.

## In Simple Language
Certain types of income are treated as having been received in a particular tax year, even if they were not physically handed over in cash at that exact moment. Section 7 lists these specific incomes, which include certain provident fund balances, employer pension contributions, and company dividends.

## Key Points
* Annual accretion to an employee's recognized provident fund balance to the extent provided in paragraph 6 of Part A of Schedule XI is deemed received in the tax year.
* Transferred balance in a recognized provident fund to the extent provided in paragraph 11(4) and (5) of Part A of Schedule XI is deemed received in the tax year.
* Contributions made by the Central Government or any other employer in the tax year to an employee's account under a pension scheme mentioned in section 124 are deemed received.
* Dividends declared, distributed, or paid by a company within the meaning of section 2(40)(a) to (e) are deemed to be the income of the tax year in which they are so declared, distributed, or paid.
* Interim dividends are deemed to be the income of the tax year in which the amount is unconditionally made available by the company to the entitled member.

## Who Does This Apply To?
This section applies to:
* Employees participating in a recognized provident fund.
* Employees for whom contributions are made by the Central Government or any other employer under a pension scheme mentioned in section 124.
* Assessees who are entitled to receive dividends from a company.

## Conditions
* Provident fund accretions and transferred balances must meet the conditions and limits provided in the respective paragraphs of Part A of Schedule XI.
* Employer contributions must be made in the relevant tax year under a pension scheme mentioned in section 124.
* Dividends must fall within the meaning of section 2(40)(a) to (e) or be unconditionally made available in the case of interim dividends.

## Exceptions
The official text of section 7 does not provide any exceptions.

## Practical Example
*Practical Example (Illustrative Assumption)*: Suppose a company declares a dividend on March 15 of a tax year, and it is paid to the shareholder on April 5 of the next financial year. Under section 7(2)(a), because the dividend is declared within the tax year, it is deemed to be the income of the tax year in which it was declared.

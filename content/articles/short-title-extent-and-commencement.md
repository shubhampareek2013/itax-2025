## Official Provision
* **Act:** Income-tax Act, 2025
* **Section:** 1
* **Chapter:** Chapter I — Preliminary
* **Effective Date:** 1st April, 2026 (save as otherwise provided in the Act)

Section 1 establishes the official name of the legislation as the Income-tax Act, 2025, specifies that its provisions extend to the whole of India, and dictates that the Act comes into force on the 1st April, 2026, except as otherwise provided in the Act.

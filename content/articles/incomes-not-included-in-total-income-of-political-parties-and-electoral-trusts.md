## Official Provision
* Act: Income-tax Act, 2025
* Section: 12
* Chapter: Chapter III — Incomes which do not form part of total income
* Effective date: As per the official provisions of the Income-tax Act, 2025.

Section 12 provides that in computing the total income of any political party or an electoral trust for a tax year, any income enumerated in Schedule VIII shall not be included, subject to the fulfillment of conditions specified in that Schedule. If the conditions referred to in Schedule VIII are not satisfied in any tax year, such income is charged to tax for that tax year. Additionally, the Central Government may make rules or issue notifications for the purposes of this section as specified in Schedule VIII.

## In Simple Language
When calculating the taxable income of a political party or an electoral trust, specific incomes listed in Schedule VIII are left out, provided all the rules and conditions mentioned in that Schedule are strictly met. If those conditions are broken or not met during a tax year, the income that was supposed to be excluded will become fully taxable for that year. The Central Government also has the power to issue rules and notifications concerning this section as outlined in Schedule VIII.

## Key Points
* Excludes income enumerated in Schedule VIII for political parties and electoral trusts from total income.
* Exemption or non-inclusion is strictly subject to the fulfillment of conditions specified in Schedule VIII.
* Non-satisfaction of conditions in any tax year results in the income being charged to tax for that tax year.
* Empowers the Central Government to make rules or issue notifications as specified in Schedule VIII.

## Who Does This Apply To?
* Political parties
* Electoral trusts

## Conditions
* Fulfillment of conditions specified in Schedule VIII for the relevant tax year.

## Exceptions
* If conditions referred to in Schedule VIII are not satisfied in any tax year, the income enumerated in the Schedule is charged to tax for that tax year.

## Practical Example
*Practical Example (Illustrative only, not a legal limit):* Suppose a political party earns rental or voluntary contribution income covered under Schedule VIII and fully complies with the conditions specified therein during the tax year; such income is excluded from its total income. If the party fails to satisfy a condition required under Schedule VIII in a subsequent tax year, that income will be charged to tax for that tax year.

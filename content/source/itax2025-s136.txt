136. (1) An assessee, being an Indian company, shall be allowed a
deduction for the amount contributed by it, other than by way of cash, during a
tax year to a political party registered under section 29A of the Representation
of the People Act, 1951 or an electoral trust.
(2) For the purposes of this section, the term “contribute”, with its
grammatical variations and cognate expressions shall have the same meaning as
assigned to it in section 182 of the Companies Act, 2013.
Deduction in respect of contributions given by any person to political
parties.

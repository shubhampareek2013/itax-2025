383. (1) An applicant desirous of obtaining an advance ruling under this
Chapter, may make an application in such form and manner, as may be
prescribed,14 stating the question on which the advance ruling is sought.
(2) The application shall be accompanied by a fee, as may be prescribed.15
(3) An applicant may withdraw an application within thirty days from the date
of the application.

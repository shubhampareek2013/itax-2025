497. The Special Court, irrespective of anything contained in the Bharatiya
Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, shall try an offence under this Chapter
punishable with imprisonment not exceeding two years or with fine, or with both,
as a summons case, and the provisions of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita,
2023 as applicable in the case of trial of summons case, shall apply accordingly.
Application of Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 to proceedings
before Special Court.

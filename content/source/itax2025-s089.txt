89. Irrespective of anything contained in sections 82, 83, 84, 85 and 86,––
(a) if the transfer of the original asset mentioned in those sections is
by way of compulsory acquisition under any law; and
(b) if the compensation awarded for such acquisition is not received
by the assessee on the date of transfer, then, the period available to him
under those sections for acquisition of the new asset or investment or
deposit of capital gain in specified bank or institution shall be reckoned
from the date of receipt of compensation.
Meaning of “adjusted”, “cost of improvement” and “cost of acquisition”.
